# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Mara, 38, the consumer (embodies "giving up")
Role: A shift worker with variable income and two open claims, who handles her debt from her phone in the gaps of her day.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Find an affordable instalment plan she can trust, and set it up herself without having to explain her finances to an agent.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** She opens the portal from a reminder, reaches plan setup, and can't tell what monthly amount is safe. If she tries a custom amount, she may hit "Unable to process request" with no guidance . If she pauses on her phone, the session can expire after 5 minutes and wipe her data . Either way, she is offered no link to resume. She then calls an agent, or forgets and never returns.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** She calls customer support or sends an email, because it "felt easier".
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Opens the portal from the reminder on her phone, in a short gap between shifts.
Looks for a plan option. She is routed to information pages, or the option is hidden by the debt-threshold defect.
Tries to choose a monthly amount but can't tell what is safe, and doesn't want to experiment.
Hits a dead end. A custom amount may trigger "Unable to process request" with no guidance , or her mobile session times out and wipes her data.
Has no way back in. No continuation link is offered.
